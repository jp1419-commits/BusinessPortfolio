Privacy Policy

Receipt Tracker · last updated 17 September 2026

Receipt Tracker is a mobile application for capturing receipts and preparing them for BIR reporting in the Philippines. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

Receipt Tracker is currently in closed beta. It is being tested by a small group of invited users. Data created during the beta may be deleted without notice as the product changes.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, these come from your Google account, and we receive nothing else from Google — no contacts, no calendar, no files.
  • Your password, if you create an account with one. It is stored only as a one-way hash and cannot be read back by anyone, ourselves included. Accounts created with Google have no password.
  • Receipts you record. Merchant name and address, supplier TIN, invoice or receipt number, dates, amounts, VAT figures, expense category and any notes you add.
  • Receipt images you photograph or upload.
  • Sign-in records. The time you signed in, your IP address and your device's browser identification, kept so that unexpected access to your account can be investigated.

What we do not collect

  • Payment card details. The beta takes no payments.
  • Location data, contacts, messages, or anything else on your device outside the images you choose to add.
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers. There is no advertising in this app and no third-party analytics.

Where it is kept

Your data is stored in Microsoft Azure, in the Southeast Asia region (Singapore), and in the app's own storage on your device. The app is offline-first: receipts are recorded on your device and sent to the server when a connection is available.

Who can see it

Your receipts and account details are visible to you, and to other users of your own workspace if you invite any. They are not sold, rented or shared with advertisers or data brokers. They are disclosed to others only where the law requires it.

The infrastructure is operated by Microsoft Azure and, for sign-in, Google. Both act as service providers and neither is given your receipt data for its own purposes.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. Ask us to delete your account and everything belonging to it is removed, including receipts, images and sign-in records.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted.
  • Export your receipts to Excel from inside the app at any time.
  • Stop using Google sign-in by removing this app's access in your Google account permissions.

Children

Receipt Tracker is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
